Understanding Energy Consumption

Household appliances contribute significantly to residential electricity consumption. Understanding how much energy appliances use can help Australian households make more informed purchasing and energy-saving decisions.

Appliances are rated for the power they draw, measured in watts. The energy an appliance actually uses depends on that rating and on how long the appliance runs, which is why two similar products can have very different running costs.

Power rating

The watts an appliance draws while it is operating. A higher rating means more energy is used for the same running time.

Usage time

Hours of use per day. A low-powered appliance left running all day can still use more energy than a high-powered one used briefly.

Electricity price

Retail tariffs are usually quoted in cents per kilowatt hour, and they vary between states and retailers.
